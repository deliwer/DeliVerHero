Slide 2 – The Climate Challenge

Header: Pakistan: Among the Most Climate-Affected Nations

Key Points:

33 million people impacted by floods (2022–2023)

Severe water stress and deforestation accelerating

Diaspora-led environmental engagement needed urgently

🎙 Speaker Notes:

In 2022–23, catastrophic floods displaced over 33 million Pakistanis.

Pakistan now ranks among the top 10 most climate-vulnerable nations — not because of what we emit, but because of where we live.

Our diaspora, especially here in the UAE, has both the capacity and the compassion to act.

PAD Planet Hero is our answer to that call — blending community spirit with measurable impact.

Slide 3 – Mission Overview

Header: Introducing the PAD Planet Hero Mission

Key Points:

Joint initiative by PAD and DeliWer

Focus on Tree Plantation, Water Conservation, Flood Relief Awareness

Digital participation via Planet Points Leaderboard

🎙 Speaker Notes:

The PAD Planet Hero Mission connects environmental activism with digital engagement.

Every volunteer earns “Planet Points” — rewards that recognize environmental action through the DeliWer leaderboard.

Whether planting trees, attending workshops, or contributing to flood relief, every action is tracked and celebrated.

It’s a movement where technology and community meet for a shared cause.

🌱 Executive Summary

The PAD Planet Hero Mission unites Pakistan’s global diaspora for actionable environmental impact through tree plantation, water conservation, and flood relief awareness.

It leverages community engagement, digital gamification (Planet Points), and CSR partnerships to drive measurable outcomes.

Through this initiative, PAD and DeliWer position Pakistanis abroad as champions of sustainability, reinforcing the message:

“Our roots may be in different soils, but our mission is one — to heal the planet we call home.”
