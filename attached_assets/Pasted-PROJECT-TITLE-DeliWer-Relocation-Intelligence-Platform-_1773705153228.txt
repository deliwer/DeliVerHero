PROJECT TITLE
DeliWer Relocation Intelligence Platform – FINAL MASTER PROMPT (Ultimate)

OBJECTIVE
Transform deliwer.com into Dubai’s **leading relocation coordination and intelligence platform** capturing tenant demand during key relocation events:

• registering Ejari
• renewing tenancy
• receiving rent increase notices
• discovering overpayment
• moving apartments
• leaving Dubai

The platform must generate relocation coordination leads for DeliWer and its partner ecosystem while providing actionable insights to tenants about **when and where to move**.

CRITICAL RULES

Do NOT restructure the existing website or remove content.

Enhance the current structure with pages, tools, calculators, and relocation funnels.

System must be static (no database required).

All coordination requests must route through WhatsApp.

Referral tracking works via URL parameters and client-side JavaScript.
