PROJECT TITLE
DeliWer Partnership Model & Distribution Engine – Master Prompt

OBJECTIVE
Transform deliwer.com into a **partner-first growth platform** where all customer acquisition is driven by:

• strategic partners
• distribution partners
• affiliate referrals

The system must enable DeliWer to scale **without marketing spend**, by turning existing partner networks into revenue-generating channels.

---

CORE STRATEGIC PRINCIPLE

DeliWer does NOT acquire customers directly.

DeliWer acquires **partners who already own customer relationships**.

---

PARTNERSHIP POSITIONING

DeliWer must be positioned as:

A **monetization layer on top of existing tenant interactions**

Partners are NOT selling DeliWer.

Partners are:

Enhancing their existing conversations and earning additional income.

---

PRIMARY PARTNER CATEGORIES

1. STRATEGIC PARTNERS (35% Revenue Share)

Who:

• Real estate brokerages
• Ejari typing centers
• Corporate relocation companies
• Building management companies

Value they bring:

• direct tenant access
• high-intent demand
• recurring volume

Messaging:

“Unlock a new income stream from your existing tenant pipeline.”

---

2. DISTRIBUTION PARTNERS (20–25%)

Who:

• individual brokers
• leasing agents
• building staff
• small relocation agents

Value:

• direct conversations with tenants
• localized influence

Messaging:

“Earn from every tenant interaction without extra effort.”

---

3. INFLUENCERS / COMMUNITY PARTNERS (10–15%)

Who:

• social media influencers
• WhatsApp group admins
• community leaders

Value:

• reach and trust

Messaging:

“Help your audience save money and earn per referral.”

---

4. GENERAL REFERRAL TIER (5–10%)

Who:

• any user

---

PARTNER JOURNEY FLOW

Partner onboarding must follow this flow:

Discover DeliWer
→ Understand earning potential
→ Get referral link
→ Share with network
→ Generate leads
→ Track performance
→ Receive earnings
→ Increase activity
