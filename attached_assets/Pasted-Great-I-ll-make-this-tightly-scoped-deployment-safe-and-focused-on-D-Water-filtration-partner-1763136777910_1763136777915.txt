## Key features & UX details (incremental, non-invasive)

### 1) Homepage entry points (non-destructive)

* Keep original hero and first fold intact; do **not** replace or rearrange.

### 2) Trade-in microflow (fast path)

* Step 1: Model → storage → condition → show instant quote (from `iphoneQuotes.json`).
* Step 2: Choose reward: Cash / DeliPoints / AquaCafe credit. If Cash chosen → proceed to Stripe/PayPal checkout. If DeliPoints → convert immediately to PIC in wallet.
* Step 3: Schedule pickup (simple date/time & area dropdown) → record in DB → show confirmation + admin notification.
* Minimal form fields for conversion (name, phone, area) — keep friction low.

### 3) Impact-support microflow (Dubai Municipality filtration initiative)

* Offer **value-exchange** products: “Filter Kit”, “Community Filtration Install”, or “PIC purchase” (packaged impact credits).
* Payment via PayPal (primary for quick conversion) and Stripe as alternate.
* On successful payment, create a partner task: `partner_certify` with details to be validated by the partner/admin.
* After partner confirms install, issue a public certificate (PDF) and credit user’s PIC wallet with a verification badge.
* Show impact counters on the page: kits funded; liters filtered; active partner installations (pulled from admin verified records).

### 4) Partner flow with Dubai Municipality (compliant)

* Publicly: show **“In partnership with Dubai Municipality”** only if signed/approved. If not yet signed, show “Official campaign with municipal collaboration pending” or “Supported by local municipality initiatives” — be careful to avoid legal misrepresentation. (Admin must confirm copy.)
* Technical: Add partner onboarding area in Admin to upload partner verification tokens and confirm installations. Partners can upload proof (photos, geotag, certificate). Admin verifies and marks complete.

### 5) PIC (Planet Impact Credits) economics (starter)

* Define base conversions in Admin: e.g., 1 AED → 10 PIC (configurable).
* Trade-in conversion: option to convert quote to PIC at a small bonus multiplier (e.g., 1.05x) to encourage platform credit redemptions.
* PIC cap / expiry policy: configurable in Admin.

## Payments & payouts

* **Stripe**: use hosted Stripe Checkout sessions for card payments. Create server endpoint `/api/checkout/stripe` to create sessions and return session URL.
* **PayPal**: use PayPal Checkout / Smart Buttons for fast express checkout; create `/api/checkout/paypal` for create-order and capture workflows.
* **Payouts for trade-ins (cash to seller)**: Option A (lowest friction) — issue instant bank transfer via a payment partner OR Option B — send payout as **PayPal transfer** or as store credit only initially to reduce payout friction. Implement admin review step for cash payouts to reduce fraud.
