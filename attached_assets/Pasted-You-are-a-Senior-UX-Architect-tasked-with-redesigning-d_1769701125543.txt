You are a Senior UX Architect tasked with redesigning deliwer.com to implement a **generalized concierge-first model** for all high-friction home and relocation services.

Objectives:
- Position DeliWer as a **planning & coordination layer** (Concierge) rather than a marketplace or movers service.
- Canonicalize Move-In / Move-Out packages and avoid repetition across pages.
- Differentiate Maintenance Concierge and Resident Support Concierge.
- Integrate concierge methodology into all relevant services.
- Optimize the funnel for WhatsApp-first high-intent lead capture.
- Reduce cognitive load and improve survival-grade conversion.

---

1️⃣ HOMEPAGE HERO

Headline:
“Your Home, Coordinated.”

Subheadline:
“Planning, scheduling, and managing everything around your home and move — without you juggling vendors.”

Trust line (small):
“Not movers. Not contractors. Just a single point of contact for your home.”

Primary CTA:
“Start Your Concierge” → opens WhatsApp or scrolls to /residents section.

Visual:
- Abstract planning / checklist / calendar icons
- Avoid moving trucks, boxes, labor imagery

---

2️⃣ SERVICE CARDS (Homepage + /residents page)

Only 3 primary cards for simplicity:

CARD 1 — HOME MAINTENANCE

Title:
Fix Something at Home

Description:
“For issues or repairs around your home. We coordinate trusted service partners on your behalf.”

Examples (bullets):
• AC, plumbing, electrical  
• Minor home repairs  

CTA:
“Book Maintenance”

---

CARD 2 — RESIDENT CONCIERGE (Move-In / Move-Out / Coordination Layer)

Title:
Move-In / Move-Out Concierge

Tag (small):
Planning & Coordination Layer

Description:
“We manage the planning and vendor coordination around your move — before, during, and after moving day. No trucks, no packing, just orchestration.”

Includes (micro-bullets):
• Utility connections & disconnections  
• Home setup or clearance planning  
• Vendor scheduling & timelines  
• Furniture / appliance coordination  
• Single point of contact  

Microcopy (important):
“This is not a movers service. We manage the process, not the truck.”

Primary CTA:
“Plan My Move” → opens popup → scrolls to canonical `/relocate` package section

Secondary helper text:
“Already have movers? We coordinate around them.”

---

CARD 3 — RESIDENT SUPPORT CONCIERGE

Title:
Resident Support

Description:
“For residents already living in Dubai who need help managing home setup, upgrades, or transitions.”

Examples:
• Home setup changes  
• Coordination of ongoing home improvements  
• Exit or replacement support  

CTA:
“View Support Services”

---

3️⃣ POPUP / SCROLL LOGIC

When user clicks any concierge card:

- Trigger popup that prequalifies intent
- Buttons scroll automatically to canonical package sections on /relocate or /residents
- Example buttons:
   - “I’m Moving In” → /relocate/move-in  
   - “I’m Moving Out” → /relocate/move-out  
   - “I need Resident Support” → /residents#support  

Microcopy:
“We plan, coordinate, and manage the process — not the truck.”

---

4️⃣ SEGMENT & PACKAGE CLARIFICATION

- **Move-In Concierge** → Relocating residents  
- **Move-Out Concierge** → Exiting / relocation  
- **Maintenance Concierge** → Existing residents (reactive issues)  
- **Resident Support Concierge** → Existing residents (proactive coordination, upgrades)

**Key UX Differentiation:**
- Maintenance: “We fix it for you”  
- Resident Support: “We coordinate it for you”  
- Move-In / Move-Out: “We plan & orchestrate your move”  

**Visual differentiation:**  
- Distinct icons, card colors, and headings  
- Avoid repeating Move-In/Out in multiple cards

---

5️⃣ LOYALTY / RESIDENT PRIVILEGES

- Section at bottom of /residents
- Microcopy: “Available after your first DeliWer service.”
- Perks examples:
   • Free Shower Filter installation  
   • Partner F&B vouchers  
   • Priority scheduling  
- CTA: “Unlock with Your Next Service” → scrolls back to concierge cards

---

6️⃣ CONVERSION & WHATSAPP LEAD FLOW

- Homepage + /residents → concierge card click → popup → scroll to canonical package → WhatsApp lead capture  
- WhatsApp tag identifies segment (Maintenance / Move-In / Move-Out / Resident Support)  
- Response within same day → assessment → optional premium upsell → closure  
- Loyalty / Resident Privileges activated **after first completed service**

---

7️⃣ VISUAL & UX GUIDELINES

- Mobile-first, scannable microcopy
- Minimalist design, subtle backgrounds  
- Concierge card: premium / planning-focused icons  
- Maintenance card: practical icons  
- Resident Support card: supportive icons  
- Emphasize **“single point of contact”** and **time-saving**  

---

8️⃣ SUCCESS METRICS

- Reduction of repeated Move-In/Out mentions  
- Clear differentiation between Maintenance vs Resident Support vs Move Concierge  
- High-intent, WhatsApp-initiated leads captured  
- Post-service loyalty adoption  
- Lower cognitive load and higher conversion across all segments
