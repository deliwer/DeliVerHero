"Nobody explains what Ejari actually is when you move to Dubai."

[Content — 4–50s]
"Ejari is Dubai's mandatory tenancy registration system, run by RERA — the Real Estate Regulatory Agency.

Every tenancy contract in Dubai must be registered on Ejari before you can activate DEWA electricity and water, apply for a residence visa, or legally occupy the property.

To register, you need: the signed tenancy contract, your Emirates ID, the landlord's passport or Emirates ID, and the title deed.

Once registered, you get an Ejari certificate with a unique reference number — which you then use for DEWA activation.

The whole process takes 1–3 business days. Miss one document and it starts over.
