What does it mean Play to Earn

Play to Earn exactly means "Earnings by playing".

Nowadays you have the opportunity to earn while playing and having fun.

Play to Earn gives players ownership of the game's resources (objects, characters, resources ...), these can be: found, bought or created within the game and can then be exchanged with other players or sold, where possible , in cryptocurrencies or in current money.

The metaverse is a virtual reality, shared via the internet, where every single person is represented through a 3D avatar.

The metaverse gives space to new iterations between people and a new frontier of gaming
