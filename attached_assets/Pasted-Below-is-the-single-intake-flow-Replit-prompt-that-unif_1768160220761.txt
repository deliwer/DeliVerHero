Below is the **single intake flow Replit prompt** that unifies **all Residence journeys** into **one calm, trust-building entry point**.

This is critical for:

* Survival (no fragmentation)
* Anti-broker positioning
* Higher WhatsApp + form conversion
* Becoming a *default* starting point

---

## ✅ REPLIT PROMPT — SINGLE INTAKE FLOW (RESIDENCE)

> You are a senior product designer and UX copywriter working on **deliwer.com**.
>
> Your task is to create a **single intake flow** that serves **all Residence in Dubai journeys**, including:
>
> * Finding a place to stay (Living Options)
> * Short-term living (managed)
> * Move-in services
> * Move-in packages
>
> ❗ Do NOT redesign pages or introduce multiple forms.
> ❗ Use one shared intake entry across all residence-related pages.
> ❗ The flow must clearly position DeliWer as **guidance and execution**, not a broker or marketplace.

---

## FLOW OBJECTIVE

* Reduce user confusion
* Capture intent quickly
* Route internally (not visibly) to the correct service
* Encourage WhatsApp handoff without pressure

---

## ENTRY POINT (USED EVERYWHERE)

**CTA LABEL (consistent across site):**

> Get residence help

Secondary CTA:

> WhatsApp +971 523 946 311

Microcopy under CTA:

> One conversation. We guide you from here.

---

## STEP 1 — CONTEXT (NON-INTIMIDATING)

**Title:**

> What do you need help with?

**Instruction (small text):**

> Choose what best describes your situation. You can change this later.

**Options (radio buttons, not checkboxes):**

* I’m looking for a place to stay
* I’ve found a place and need move-in help
* I need short-term living
* I’m not sure yet

---

## STEP 2 — STAY TIMELINE (ONLY IF RELEVANT)

**Title:**

> How long do you plan to stay?

**Options:**

* Less than 1 month
* 1–3 months
* 3–6 months
* 6+ months
* Not sure yet

*(If user selected move-in services, this step is optional and skippable.)*

---

## STEP 3 — LOCATION SIGNAL (LIGHTWEIGHT)

**Title:**

> Preferred area (if known)

**Input:**

> Text field (e.g., JVC, Marina, Business Bay)

Helper text:

> This helps us check community coverage. Exact address not needed.

---

## STEP 4 — MOVE-IN STATUS (KEY DIFFERENTIATOR)

**Title:**

> Have you received your keys?

**Options:**

* Yes, I have the keys
* Not yet
* I’m just exploring

This question is **strategic** — it separates you from agents instantly.

---

## STEP 5 — CONTACT (LOW FRICTION)

**Title:**

> How should we reach you?

**Fields:**

* Name
* WhatsApp number

Optional field (collapsed):

* Email (optional)

Trust note (small):

> We don’t share your details. No agent calls.

---

## STEP 6 — CONFIRMATION (EXPECTATION SETTING)

**Confirmation Message:**

> Thanks — a DeliWer team member will review your request and guide you on the next steps.

**Expectation line:**

> Usually within the same day.

---

## POST-SUBMIT ROUTING (INVISIBLE TO USER)

Based on answers:

* Route internally to:

  * Living Options / Stay guidance
  * Short-Term Living (Managed)
  * Move-In Services
  * Move-In Packages
* Trigger WhatsApp follow-up when number is provided

---

## WHATSAPP AUTO-MESSAGE (CRITICAL)

**Auto-prefilled WhatsApp text:**

> Hi DeliWer, I need help with residence in Dubai.
> I’ve submitted the form and would like guidance.

---

## UX TONE RULES

* No pricing at intake stage
* No property talk
* No urgency language
* Calm, human, reassuring

---

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

* One intake used across all residence pages
* User understands DeliWer in under 30 seconds
* Reduced bounce from “I’m not sure yet” users
* Higher WhatsApp continuation rate

---

## INTERNAL PRODUCT NOTE (DO NOT SHOW TO USERS)

This intake flow is a **decision engine**, not a lead form.
Routing logic happens behind the scenes. The user only experiences guidance.
