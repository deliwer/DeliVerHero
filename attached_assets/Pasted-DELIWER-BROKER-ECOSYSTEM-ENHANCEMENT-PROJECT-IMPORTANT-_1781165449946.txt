PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Expand the existing Broker Master Database and recruitment ecosystem inside the existing DeliWer platform.

The broker ecosystem is intended to become the primary growth and commission-generation engine for DeliWer and its aggregated services.

The enhancement must consolidate all broker and partner recruitment funnels under:

/marketing

while maintaining separation between different partner categories.
