PROJECT TITLE
DeliWer Relocation Concierge Funnel Enhancement

OBJECTIVE
Enhance deliwer.com to position DeliWer as a UAE Relocation Concierge Platform while preserving the existing structure, pages, and navigation. The goal is to capture high-intent relocation traffic, coordinate partners, and generate relocation leads without requiring database infrastructure.

The update must NOT restructure the website or remove existing content. Instead it should enhance conversion, create relocation funnels, and add lead capture flows.

PRIMARY VALUE PROPOSITION
“Move, arrive, or exit Dubai without the chaos. DeliWer coordinates everything.”

TARGET CUSTOMER MOMENTS
The website must capture three relocation scenarios:

1. Moving into a new home
2. Moving within Dubai/UAE to reduce rent or upgrade
3. Leaving Dubai or the UAE quickly

NEW POSITIONING
DeliWer becomes a Relocation Concierge Platform coordinating:

• Movers
• Ejari registration
• DEWA activation or closure
• Cleaning services
• Storage providers
• Maintenance teams
• Water readiness and air filter setup

No services are directly operated by DeliWer. The platform coordinates trusted partners and collects referral margins.

HOMEPAGE ENHANCEMENTS

Add a relocation decision funnel above the fold without removing existing content.

Headline:
“Moving Home in Dubai? Relax. We Handle It.”

Subheadline:
“From Ejari to movers, cleaning, utilities and water readiness — everything coordinated in one place.”

Add three primary action buttons:

1. Moving Into a New Home
2. Moving to a Cheaper Rent
3. Leaving Dubai / Exit Service

Each button opens a guided relocation flow.
