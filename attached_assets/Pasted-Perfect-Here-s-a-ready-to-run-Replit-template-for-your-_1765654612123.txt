### **2. Steps to Deploy on Replit**

1. **Create a new Replit Node.js project** → `deliwer-relocate-membership`
2. **Upload all files** from the structure above
3. **Run `npm install`** in Replit shell (auto-installs Express, EJS, Body-Parser, Express-Session)
4. **Start the server** → `node app.js`
5. Replit provides a live URL: e.g., `https://deliwer-relocate-membership.username.repl.co`
6. In your **DNS provider**, create a CNAME:

   ```
   relocate.deliwer.com → deliwer-relocate-membership.username.repl.co
   ```
7. Access `/relocate` (existing gateway) and `/community` (membership portal)
