PROJECT TITLE
DeliWer Relocation Intelligence Platform – FINAL MASTER PROMPT (Extended)

OBJECTIVE
Enhance deliwer.com to become Dubai’s leading relocation coordination and relocation intelligence platform capturing tenant demand during the key relocation moments:

• registering Ejari
• renewing tenancy
• receiving rent increase notices
• discovering they are overpaying rent
• moving apartments
• leaving Dubai

The platform must guide tenants through these situations and convert them into relocation coordination leads for DeliWer and its partner ecosystem.

CRITICAL RULES

Do NOT restructure the existing website or remove existing content.

Enhance the current structure with new pages, tools, and relocation funnels.

The system must function as a static website without requiring a database.

All coordination requests must route through WhatsApp.

Referral tracking must use URL parameters and client-side JavaScript.
