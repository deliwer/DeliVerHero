The objective is to reposition ChainTrack around a unique procurement model for pre-owned iPhones.

CURRENT MARKET PROBLEM

Wholesale buyers spend significant time:

* Monitoring multiple supplier portals
* Watching auctions
* Tracking inventory fluctuations
* Contacting different wholesalers
* Waiting for suitable lots to appear
* Comparing grades and specifications
* Missing opportunities due to timing

This creates procurement uncertainty.

CHAINTRACK'S UNIQUE ADVANTAGE

ChainTrack continuously scans and aggregates inventory from multiple suppliers.

Instead of buyers chasing inventory, buyers submit demand.

Example:

"I need 500 iPhone 13 units Grade B."

ChainTrack searches available inventory and presents matching offers.

Suppliers compete to fulfill the requirement.

The buyer receives multiple offers through reverse bidding.

The result:

* Continuous supply availability
* Reduced procurement effort
* Faster sourcing cycles
* Lower purchasing costs
* Less dependence on individual suppliers

CORE POSITIONING

ChainTrack transforms fragmented wholesale inventory into a continuously available sourcing network.
