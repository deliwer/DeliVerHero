ROLE MAP (WHO DOES WHAT)## A) DeliWer (Platform Operator)Responsibilities:* attract tenants (ads, SEO, WhatsApp funnels)* qualify users (budget, location, urgency)* match users to verified listings via licensed brokers* coordinate move-in lifecycle* provide optional services (furnishing, setup, relocation)❌ Cannot:* issue tenancy contracts* negotiate final rent as broker* collect brokerage commission directly from landlord---## B) Licensed Broker / Brokerage FirmResponsibilities:* list properties legally* negotiate rental terms* sign agreements (Ejari)* ensure compliance with RERA rules* collect brokerage commissionThey are your **legal execution layer for transactions**---## C) Landlord / Property OwnerResponsibilities:* approve occupancy type (including shared/co-living if allowed)* ensure legal occupancy limits* allow furnishing/partitioning if applicable* register tenancy through Ejari system---## D) Service ProvidersResponsibilities:* furnish rooms* install utilities* manage cleaning & maintenance* handle move-in readinessThey are **not real estate actors**, only service vendors.---## E) Freelance Agents (Important in your model)They can:* generate leads* pre-qualify tenants* refer clients to licensed brokersThey cannot:* independently close deals* issue contracts* act outside brokerage supervisionThey must be attached to a licensed firm under RERA rules.---# 3.
