You are a Senior UX Architect tasked with redesigning deliwer.com to implement a **generalized concierge-first model** for high-friction home and relocation services.

Objectives:
- Position DeliWer as a **planning & coordination layer** (Concierge) rather than a marketplace or movers service.
- Generalize the Move Concierge methodology to all services where fragmented vendors create stress (Maintenance, Move-In/Move-Out, Resident Support, Home Upgrades).
- Maintain a **high-intent, low-cognitive-load funnel** for residents and relocators.
- Clearly differentiate **Concierge / orchestration layer** from execution / vendor services.
- Introduce loyalty and premium positioning without diluting focus.

---

1️⃣ HOMEPAGE HERO

Headline:
“Your Home, Coordinated.”

Subheadline:
“Planning, scheduling, and managing everything around your home and move — without you juggling vendors.”

Trust line (small):
“Not movers. Not contractors. Just a single point of contact for your home.”

Primary CTA:
“Start Your Concierge” → opens WhatsApp or scrolls to /residents section.

Visual:
- Abstract planning / calendar / checklist icons
- Avoid moving trucks, boxes, labor imagery

---

2️⃣ SERVICE ENTRY CARDS (Homepage or /residents page)

Only 3 cards for simplicity:

CARD 1 — HOME MAINTENANCE

Title:
Fix Something at Home

Description (mobile-first):
“For issues or repairs around your home. We coordinate trusted service partners on your behalf.”

Examples (bullets):
• AC, plumbing, electrical
• Minor repairs & servicing

CTA:
“Book Maintenance”

---

CARD 2 — RESIDENT CONCIERGE (NEW — replaces Move Concierge)

Title:
Move-In / Move-Out Concierge

Tag (small):
Planning & Coordination Layer

Description:
“We manage the planning and vendor coordination around your move — before, during, and after moving day. No trucks, no packing, just orchestration.”

Includes:
• Utility connections & disconnections
• Home setup and clearance planning
• Vendor scheduling & timelines
• Furniture / appliance coordination
• Single point of contact

Microcopy (important):
“This is not a movers service. We manage the process, not the truck.”

Primary CTA:
“Plan My Move” → opens popup/scrolls to relevant section on /relocate packages page

Secondary text:
“Already have movers? We coordinate around them.”

---

CARD 3 — RESIDENT SUPPORT SERVICES

Title:
Resident Support

Description:
“For residents already living in Dubai who need help managing home setup, upgrades, or transitions.”

Examples:
• Home setup changes
• Coordination of ongoing home improvements
• Exit or replacement support

CTA:
“View Support Services”

---

3️⃣ POPUP / SCROLL METHODOLOGY

When user clicks a Concierge card (Move-In / Move-Out or Resident Support):

- Trigger popup that **prequalifies intent**
- Buttons scroll automatically to relevant sections on /relocate or /residents page:
   - “I’m Moving In” → /relocate/move-in
   - “I’m Moving Out” → /relocate/move-out
   - “I need Resident Support” → /residents#support

Microcopy in popup:
“We plan, coordinate, and manage the process — not the truck.”

---

4️⃣ MICROCOPY & TRUST REINFORCEMENT

- Everywhere, emphasize:
   - **“Single point of contact”**
   - **“Planning & coordination layer”**
   - **“We manage vendors and timelines — you save time and stress”**
- Avoid any mention of trucks, packing, or labor
- Loyalty section (bottom of /residents page):
   - Resident Privileges unlocked after first booking
   - Example perks: Free Shower Filter installation, F&B vouchers, priority scheduling

---

5️⃣ WEBSITE NAVIGATION

- Hero CTA → Concierge / WhatsApp  
- Primary menu: Residents | Relocation | Business Setup | Contact  
- Service cards → internal scroll / popups → package sections  
- No standalone movers, e-waste, or vendor pages as entry points

---

6️⃣ EXPECTED OUTCOMES

- Users understand DeliWer **manages the coordination layer**, not just individual services  
- High-intent leads for concierge services arrive pre-qualified via WhatsApp  
- Lower cognitive load, higher trust  
- Loyalty and repeat usage tied to concierge value  
- Clear differentiation between:
   - Maintenance (reactive)
   - Concierge (planning & coordination)
   - Execution partners (handled behind the scenes)

---

7️⃣ VISUAL GUIDELINES

- Concierge card: premium feel, calendar/checklist icons  
- Maintenance: practical, approachable icons  
- Resident Support: neutral, supportive  
- Minimalist backgrounds to emphasize clarity  
- Mobile-first, scannable copy

---

8️⃣ SUCCESS METRICS

- Increased WhatsApp lead conversion from concierge cards  
- Reduced confusion between “moving” vs “coordination”  
- Higher repeat bookings due to loyalty and concierge recognition  
- Clear path to upsell premium coordination packages
