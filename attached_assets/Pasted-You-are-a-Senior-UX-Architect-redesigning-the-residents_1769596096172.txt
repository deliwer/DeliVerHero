You are a Senior UX Architect redesigning the /residents page for deliwer.com.

Objective:
Remove confusion between “moving services” and “coordination & planning services”.
Position DeliWer clearly as a CONCIERGE & ORCHESTRATION LAYER — not a packing or trucking company.

Key UX Principles:
- Reduce choices to 3 clear mental models
- Separate “fixing”, “planning & coordination”, and “execution partners”
- Make it obvious that DeliWer manages vendors, timelines, and compliance — not the moving truck

PAGE STRUCTURE (REQUIRED)

1. Page Hero (Simple & Clear)

Headline:
“Home Life in Dubai, Coordinated.”

Subheadline:
“We plan, coordinate, and manage everything around your move or daily home needs — so you don’t deal with multiple vendors.”

Supporting line (small):
“Not movers. Not contractors. Your home concierge.”

Primary CTA:
“Get Started on WhatsApp”

---

2. Service Cards (ONLY THREE CARDS)

CARD 1 — HOME MAINTENANCE (Keep as-is, clarify scope)

Title:
Fix Something at Home

Description:
“For everyday home issues that need fixing or servicing.”

Examples (icons or bullets):
- AC, plumbing, electrical
- Small repairs & servicing

CTA:
“Book Maintenance”

---

CARD 2 — MOVE-IN / MOVE-OUT CONCIERGE (NEW, REPLACEMENT CARD)

Title:
Move-In / Move-Out Concierge

Tag (small):
Planning & Coordination Layer

Description:
“We coordinate everything around your move — before and after moving day.
No trucks. No packing. Just orchestration.”

What this includes (bullet points):
- Utility connections & disconnections
- Home setup or clearance planning
- Furniture or appliance coordination
- Vendor scheduling & compliance support
- Single point of contact

Clarifying microcopy (important):
“This is not a movers service. We manage the process, not the truck.”

CTA (primary):
“Plan My Move”

Secondary text link:
“Already have movers? We coordinate around them.”

---

CARD 3 — RESIDENT SERVICES (Optional / Secondary)

Title:
Resident Support Services

Description:
“For residents already living in Dubai who need help managing their home.”

Examples:
- Setup changes
- Home upgrades coordination
- Exit or replacement support

CTA:
“See Resident Services”

---

3. Remove or Avoid the Following

- Do NOT show any packing, boxes, trucks, or movers imagery
- Do NOT use words like “shifting”, “relocation trucks”, or “transport”
- Do NOT list individual vendors as services
- Do NOT add pricing tables on this page

---

4. Visual & UX Notes

- Use a different icon style for the Concierge card (calendar / checklist / orchestration)
- Visually separate Concierge from Maintenance (different background or border)
- Keep copy calm, authoritative, and minimal
- Emphasize “single point of contact” repeatedly

---

5. Outcome of This Page

After reading this page, the user should clearly understand:
- Maintenance = fixing things
- Concierge = planning & coordination
- Movers = external, optional, already chosen or handled elsewhere

End result:
Lower confusion, higher trust, better-qualified WhatsApp leads.
