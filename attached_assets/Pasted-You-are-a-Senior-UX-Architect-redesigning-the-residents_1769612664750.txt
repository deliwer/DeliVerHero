You are a Senior UX Architect redesigning the /residents page for deliwer.com.

Primary Goal:
Introduce DeliWer as a CONCIERGE & COORDINATION LAYER (not movers, not contractors),
and create a clear handoff into Move-In and Move-Out packages.

Audience:
Residents already living in Dubai who are:
- fixing things at home
- planning a move (before or after moving day)
- overwhelmed by coordinating multiple vendors

DESIGN RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE):
- Mobile-first copy (short, scannable)
- Only 3 primary cards
- Explicitly say what Concierge is NOT
- No pricing, no trucks, no packing language
- Concierge must feel higher-level than maintenance

--------------------------------
PAGE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------

1. HERO SECTION

Headline:
“Your Home, Coordinated.”

Subheadline:
“We plan and manage everything around your move or home needs — without you dealing with multiple vendors.”

Trust line (small):
“Not movers. Not contractors. Your home concierge.”

Primary CTA:
“Start on WhatsApp”

--------------------------------
2. SERVICE CARDS (3 ONLY)
--------------------------------

CARD 1 — MAINTENANCE

Title:
Fix Something at Home

Description (2 lines max):
“When something breaks or needs servicing.”

Examples (icons or micro-bullets):
• AC, plumbing, electrical  
• Small home repairs  

CTA:
“Book Maintenance”

--------------------------------

CARD 2 — MOVE CONCIERGE (CORE CHANGE)

Title:
Move-In / Move-Out Concierge

Tag (small, above title):
Planning & Coordination

Description (very clear):
“We coordinate everything around your move — before and after moving day.”

What this IS (micro-bullets):
• Utility connections & disconnections  
• Home setup or clearance planning  
• Vendor scheduling & timelines  
• One point of contact  

What this is NOT (important microcopy):
“No packing. No trucks. No movers.”

Primary CTA:
“Plan My Move”

Secondary helper text (below CTA):
“Already have movers? We coordinate around them.”

--------------------------------

CARD 3 — RESIDENT SUPPORT

Title:
Resident Support

Description:
“For changes, upgrades, or transitions while living in your home.”

Examples:
• Home setup changes  
• Exit or replacement coordination  

CTA:
“View Support Services”

--------------------------------
3. HANDOFF LOGIC (CRITICAL UX)
--------------------------------

When user clicks “Plan My Move”:

Show an intermediate clarification screen or section with:

Title:
“What We Handle”

Bullets:
• Planning & coordination  
• Utilities & setup  
• Clearance & handover prep  

Divider line

Title:
“What Happens Next”

Copy:
“Based on your situation, we’ll guide you to the right package.”

Two buttons:

Button 1:
“I’m Moving In”
→ Route to /move-in package page

Button 2:
“I’m Moving Out”
→ Route to /move-out package page

Small reassurance text:
“We manage the process — not the moving truck.”

--------------------------------
4. VISUAL & CONTENT GUIDELINES
--------------------------------

- Concierge card must look more premium than Maintenance
- Use planning / checklist / calendar style icons
- Avoid images of boxes, trucks, or labor
- Keep tone calm, authoritative, non-salesy
- Emphasize “coordination” and “single point of contact”

--------------------------------
EXPECTED OUTCOME
--------------------------------

After this page:
- Users clearly understand DeliWer’s role
- Concierge is not mistaken for movers
- Move-In and Move-Out packages feel like the next logical step
- WhatsApp leads arrive pre-qualified and less price-sensitive
