# 1) Revenue model — simple, attractive, and legally transparent

Design goals: immediate seller acceptance, competitive pricing vs alternatives, and clear incentives for compliance and evidence generation.

Primary streams (mix these; each has a clear label on invoices):

1. **Transaction Commission (Core) — Competitive, tiered**

   * Base: **1.0% – 1.5% of FOB export value** for high-volume, verified OEM sellers (e.g., authorized Apple distributors).
   * Standard sellers: **1.8% – 2.5%**.
   * Rationale: low enough to attract sellers, covers platform ops + partial escrow risk.

2. **Escrow & Payment Handling Fee (Fixed + %), passed to buyer or seller as agreed**

   * Fixed per-shipment: **$50 – $150** (covers bank SWIFT/escrow fees, reconciliation).
   * Variable: **0.15% – 0.35%** of transaction for cross-border FX processing (transparent pass-through).

3. **Fast-Release Fee (Optional, financing)**

   * If seller wants earlier release beyond the standard partial release, offer an advance financing product:

     * Example: **1.5% – 3.0% APR for up to 14 days** (transparent, capped).
     * Funded by DeliWer or a banking partner; only if KYC + export documents validated.

4. **Value-Added Services (Optional add-ons)**

   * Customs brokerage / DAFZA clearance: **Fixed per shipment** (e.g., $120–$350 depending on mode).
   * Quality inspection / OEM authentication: **$30–$100 per lot**.
   * Export packaging & labeling compliance: **flat fee**.
   * Premium onboarding (priority verification): **one-time fee $200**.

5. **Subscription / Membership (optional loyalty)**

   * “Verified Exporter” plan for frequent sellers: **$50/month or $450/year** — reduces commission by 0.25% and shortens escrow hold times.

6. **Marketplace FX Spread (transparent)**

   * Show FX as a separate line item with capped spread (e.g., max 0.5%); ideally replace with pass-through FX from partner bank.

7. **Dispute / Chargeback Reserve**

   * Hold a **1% reserve** from commission for 90 days to handle claims; fully refundable if unused.

Pricing example (mock): 50 iPhones, FOB ₹55,000 each → total ₹2,750,000 (~USD 33,000).

* Transaction commission 1.5% = $495
* Escrow fixed fee = $100
* FX fee 0.2% = $66
* Customs/clearance = $200
* Total fees = $861 (clearly shown on invoice)

# 2) Escrow & Payment Flow (ensures “white money” traceability)

Principles: no cash, bank-to-bank transfers only, SWIFT traceability, remittance evidence, dual verification.

Flow:

1. Buyer (DeliWer FZCO) creates Escrow order on ChainTrack → Escrow bank partner (licensed UAE bank) opens Escrow ID.
2. Buyer deposits funds to escrow via SWIFT / AED/USD transfer. Bank returns stamped remittance advice / SWIFT MT103.
3. ChainTrack receives bank confirmation (automated bank API or manual upload + CA attestation for first N transactions).
4. Seller begins export; uploads Shipping Bill / AWB / ICEGATE reference and FIRC when payment received.
5. On verified Shipping Bill & ICEGATE match, **partial release** (e.g., 70%) to seller bank account via SWIFT (bank confirmation required).
6. On arrival & DAFZA clearance, final release (30%).
7. All releases accompanied by signed release instructions, MT103 copies, and CA-verified monthly escrow reconciliations.

Escrow fees clearly shown and paid from escrow. Keep an immutable ledger (append-only) with timestamps for audit.

# 3) Confidentiality & security (technical + contractual)

A. **Technical measures**

* **Encryption**: TLS 1.3 in transit; AES-256 at rest for documents. Keys managed by HSM or cloud KMS.
* **Auth & Access**: OAuth2 + MFA for all user logins. Role-based access control (RBAC) with least privilege.
* **Data separation**: Multi-tenant separation; seller docs isolated per account.
* **Audit logs**: Immutable tamper-evident logs for all document uploads and user actions (append-only with hash chain).
* **Backups & retention**: Encrypted backups, retention policy aligned to legal requirements (India GST: 6–8 years recommended; UAE 5–7 years — keep 7).
* **Certifications target**: SOC2 Type II and ISO 27001 (plan & timeline) — marketable and auditor-friendly.
* **Payment security**: PCI-DSS compliance if card processing; otherwise use bank tokenisation and avoid storing card data.

B. **Contractual protections**

* **Mutual NDA** between DeliWer and seller, with carve-outs for regulated reporting and law enforcement requests.
* **Limited Data Use** clause: platform may use docs only for facilitating transaction & compliance; no resale of PII.
* **Breach notification**: 72 hours notification window and remediation plan; follow UAE PDPL and GDPR timelines as applicable.
* **Audit rights**: DeliWer + appointed CA has the right to conduct periodic compliance audits; seller must cooperate.

C. **Access for regulators**

* Provide a secure “Regulatory Portal” where approved regulators / auditors (with signed NDA) can view sanitized evidence packs for inspections.

# 4) Anti-Money-Laundering & Financial Controls (prevent flags)

Operational controls:

1. **KYC / CDD at onboarding**

   * Verify GSTIN (GSTN), IEC (DGFT), PAN, company registry (MCA for India), beneficial owners (UBO declaration), and bank account ownership.
   * Sanctions & PEP screening (OFAC, EU, UN lists, local lists).

2. **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)**

   * Triggered for: high-value shipments, new counterparties, politically exposed persons, unusual volumes.
   * Require Source of Funds (SoF) & Source of Wealth (SoW) docs, audited financials, tax filings.

3. **Transaction Monitoring**

   * Automated rules: velocity checks, TXN amount thresholds, mismatch between invoice and shipping bill, repetitive cancellations, multiple sellers routing to same bank account.
   * Flag & hold if anomalies; require CA attestation or manual compliance review.

4. **Bank partner checks**

   * Use licensed banks with strong AML processes. Require banks to perform their own KYC on rail and provide bank confirmations.

5. **Record Retention & Reporting**

   * Retain full records for 7 years.
   * File Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) with local FIU as required; cooperate with regulators.

6. **Independent audit**

   * Quarterly internal controls review + annual external audit by a Big4 or reputable CA firm covering AML controls, escrow reconciliations, and KYC effectiveness.

# 5) Chartered Accountant role & compliance verification

Make a CA (or CA firm) central to the trust model:

* **Pre-launch**: CA (India) and CA (UAE) review and sign-off: accounting flows, escrow structuring, withholding tax exposure, GST refund evidence pack layout, FIRC mapping, tax reporting.

* **Monthly reconciliations**: CA produces monthly escrow reconciliations and compliance certificates; these are uploaded to ChainTrack and viewable by regulators.
* **Audit & SOC**: CA assists in preparing for SOC2/ISO27001 certification and performs the AML operational audits.
* **Tax counsel coordination**: CA coordinates with tax counsel for treaty analysis, VAT positions, and corporate tax implications for DeliWer (UAE Freezone) and sellers.

# 6) Legal safe-guards & contract clause templates (short snippets you can copy)

A. **Confidentiality / NDA (short)**

> “Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other Party and shall use such information solely to perform its obligations hereunder. Confidential Information excludes information required to be disclosed by law or regulatory authority, provided the disclosing Party is given prompt notice and the disclosure is limited.”

B. **Escrow & Payment Clause (short)**

> “Buyer shall deposit funds into Escrow. Escrow bank shall release 70% to Seller upon verified Shipping Bill and ICEGATE confirmation; final 30% upon customs clearance in DAFZA. All transfers via bank transfer (SWIFT/MT103). Platform shall provide remittance confirmations and CA attestation upon request.”

C. **Warranties & OEM Compliance (short)**

> “Seller warrants it has all necessary OEM authorisations to supply the Goods. Seller indemnifies Buyer for any claims arising from unauthorized sale, counterfeit goods, or breaches of OEM agreements.”

D. **AML & Audit Clause (short)**

> “Seller consents to KYC, transaction monitoring, and CA audits. Seller shall provide SoF/SoW documents upon request. Platform shall report suspicious transactions to FIU and cooperate with law enforcement as required.”

E. **Governing law & dispute resolution**

> “This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Dubai International Financial Centre (or UAE federal law) and any dispute shall be finally resolved by arbitration under the rules of the Dubai International Arbitration Centre (DIAC) / or LCIA in London — choose agreed neutral forum. Interim injunctive relief may be sought in UAE courts.”

# 7) Data protection across jurisdictions (India / UAE / EU)

* **India**: follow local data localization and document retention (GST / DGFT) — store copies securely; provide seller access and rights to data.
* **UAE (PDPL)**: comply with PDPL requirements on processing, consent, and cross-border transfer; publish a transparent privacy policy and DPO contact.
* **EU (GDPR)**: if processing EU personal data, designate a DPO, standard contractual clauses (SCCs) for transfers, and allow data subject rights (access, erasure) — integrate into UI workflows.

# 8) Dispute resolution & indemnities

* Maintain a small **Claims Reserve** (1% of commission) for quick remediation to sellers/buyers for documentary or customs mismatch claims.
* Arbitration clause with expedited interim relief for urgent customs / payment blocks.
* Clear indemnity waterfall: seller indemnifies for document mis-representation; DeliWer indemnifies for import duty/VAT misstatements on buyer side.
