PROJECT TITLE
DeliWer Ejari Relocation Lead Engine

OBJECTIVE
Build a high-intent relocation lead funnel around Ejari registration and Ejari cancellation processes in Dubai. The system should attract tenants who are:

• signing a new tenancy contract
• moving apartments
• cancelling Ejari before leaving UAE

The funnel captures these users and converts them into relocation coordination leads for DeliWer.

This must integrate into the existing deliwer.com structure without redesigning the entire website.

KEY STRATEGY

Ejari is mandatory for every tenancy contract in Dubai.

Every tenant must do one of these:

• Register Ejari for a new apartment
• Transfer utilities after Ejari
• Cancel Ejari when leaving

This makes Ejari the highest-intent moment for relocation services.

The website must position DeliWer as the easiest way to handle the process.
