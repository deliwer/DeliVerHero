PROJECT: DeliWer Move-In Platform Optimization

OBJECTIVE

Enhance deliwer.com to position DeliWer as the default move-in coordination platform for tenants in Dubai.

All enhancements must maintain the current homepage structure and service funnel while improving conversion, partner referrals, and vendor coordination.

--------------------------------------------------

CORE SERVICE POSITIONING

DeliWer provides a single service:

Move-In Coordination

This includes:

• movers coordination
• Ejari registration
• DEWA activation guidance
• water readiness check
• shower filter installation
• vendor scheduling

Tenants pay only normal vendor costs.

DeliWer coordination fees are embedded in vendor payouts and are not displayed to tenants.
